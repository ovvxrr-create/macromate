Safety first: grappling submissions, chokes, joint locks, throws and striking can cause serious injury. Train with a qualified coach and consenting partner, use mats and appropriate protection, never apply a technique suddenly, and release immediately when someone taps or shows distress. This guide is educational, not a substitute for hands-on coaching.

How to train safely

Beginners: start with stance, breakfalls, movement, controlled positional drills and technique on pads or compliant partners. Increase resistance gradually with your instructor.

Submissions: first learn position, control, the tap signal and safe release. Avoid high-force finishing mechanics and never practice neck restraints alone.

Striking: begin with shadowboxing and pads. Keep contact controlled, and prioritize defense and footwork before sparring.

Progress: practice 2–4 movements per session, record coach feedback, and revisit fundamentals regularly.
